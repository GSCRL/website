About GSCRL

Garden State Combat Robotics Inc. is an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (Federal EIN: 41-2283691; NJ entity ID: 0451363992) that puts on combat robotics events for folks local and traveling to build and fight their best bots! Events are held live during our competition season, and streamed online. Competitors give it their all to go home with trophies, prizes, and bragging rights, and you make it all possible.

Mission Statement

The Garden State Combat Robotics League (GSCRL) sparks passion in anyone with an interest in robotics, no matter their skill or background, by harnessing the educational power of combat robotics to build and fight robots in fun, high-energy competitions. Through experiential learning, we foster practical skills and problem-solving, cultivating a supportive, inclusive community—both in-person and online—where individual creativity thrives. We learn from wins and losses to fuel personal growth, excitement, and connection among competitors, volunteers, and spectators.

Vision Statement

The Garden State Combat Robotics League (GSCRL) envisions a future where we reign as the unrivaled epicenter for ant-weight and plastic ant-weight combat robotics, igniting boundless creativity and learning in anyone with an interest, no matter their skill or background. We are building a vibrant, inclusive community where the educational power of robotics fuels individual innovation through hands-on robot design. We are planning to unleash your creations in 11 exhilarating annual events, including several multi-day spectacles and a grand championship at the NJ State Fair, featuring ant-weight, plastic-ant-weight, and beetle competitions. Our Beetle events provide a welcoming robot proving ground that hones competitors’ skills and provides a proven launchpad for success with Beetle and even more powerful robots at NHRL. As a nonprofit, we dedicate our resources to advancing robotics education, forging a tight-knit community that cheers every participant, redefines the sport with bold creations, and inspires a new generation to shape the future of robotics.

Purpose

  • Use our nonprofit resources exclusively to promote combat robotics and robotics education.
  • Ignite individual creativity by empowering everyone to build robots and gain practical skills through hands-on exploration.
  • Deliver a fun, accessible stage for anyone to compete and learn through thrilling robot fights.
  • Build a tight-knit community that cheers every participant and forges bonds through a shared excitement for robotics.
  • Grow competitors’ skills, creativity, and confidence.
